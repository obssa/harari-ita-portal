# Mentors and tech volunteers

For staff who coordinate mentors and tech volunteers (by default the Innovation Officer, over the directorates or
offices they look after). Amharic version: [05-mentors-volunteers.am.md](05-mentors-volunteers.am.md).

Who: **Mentors and volunteers** in the admin.

People offer their skills at `/{language}/volunteer`: what they can offer (mentoring startups, software, data,
networks, teaching digital skills and more), when they are free, the languages they speak and, if they wish,
the directorate or office they would like to help. You get a notice for each sign-up in the units you look
after; the list shows a number for **New** sign-ups.

1. Call the person, then open their page. **Add a task** at an event (the task takes the event's name and
   date) or on its own, for example mentoring a startup team. The first task makes a new sign-up **Active**.
2. After the task, **Update** it to **Done** with the hours given. The hours are added up on the list.
3. **Change status** to **Inactive** when someone stops. Tasks are cancelled, never deleted.
4. **Add a note** for anything colleagues should know; notes are seen only by staff who can see the person.

Sign-ups nobody followed up are deleted after a year. A person can ask for a copy of their data or for it to be
deleted (**Privacy requests**, chapter 01).
