# The dashboard, reports and exports

This chapter is for all staff, and in particular for directorate heads and administrators. An Amharic version will
follow once the Agency has reviewed this English text.

## 1. Your dashboard

The dashboard is the first page of the admin. It shows only the widgets of your work: a procurement officer sees
the opportunity deadlines, an academy coordinator the next cohorts, a directorate head the projects, data,
opportunities and support of their directorate.

- **Filters** at the top: the period (7, 30, 90 or 365 days, or the Ethiopian fiscal year from 1 Hamle), a language
  and a directorate. Widgets about directorate records follow the directorate you choose.
- **Customise** adds, removes, orders and resizes your widgets; **Reset** goes back to your role's default.

The widgets added in phase 7:

- **Digital Harar at a glance:** the figures of the public numbers page, counted from the records. A number you may
  open in the admin is a link.
- **Innovation pipeline:** for each programme cohort taking or deciding applications, how many are at each stage;
  for each running challenge, how many teams submitted and how many scores judges have given. No applicant names.
- **Projects and data:** running projects by status (green, amber, red, always written out), the red and amber ones
  by name, and the datasets overdue for an update.
- **Academy: next cohorts:** places taken of places offered and the waiting list. Instructors see their own cohorts.
- **Opportunity deadlines:** what closes in the next seven days and the latest entries of the logs.

## 2. The applications board

Who may use it: those who see applications (innovation officers, administrators).

**Innovation → Applications → Applications board** shows one cohort's applications as columns by stage (received,
screening, interview, accepted, waitlisted, declined), the best scored first. Choose the cohort at the top. Open a
card to move the application: the decision needs the right permission and a note the applicant reads.

## 3. Exports

Each of these lists has **Export CSV** at the top. The file keeps the filters you set, opens in any spreadsheet with
Ethiopic text intact, and every export is recorded in the activity log (who, when, which list and how many rows; not
the data).

- **Opportunities** and **Datasets**: for those who see them; directorate heads get their directorates' only.
- **Startups**: the directory, without the founders' contact details.
- **A cohort's learners** (on the cohort, under Learners): names, phones and emails, so only staff who manage cohorts
  have it. Keep the file safe and delete it when you no longer need it.

## 4. The weekly digest

Every Monday at 08:00 directorate heads receive a summary of their directorates, and administrators of the whole
Agency: support requests received, resolved and past their target; projects by status; datasets overdue;
opportunities closing in the week ahead; cohorts starting; applications waiting for a decision; content published
and waiting for review. Only what you may see in the admin, and only the parts with something to report; with
nothing to report, no digest is sent.

It arrives by email. To receive it in the portal or on Telegram instead, or not at all, open **Notifications → How I am
notified** and change **Weekly digest**.
