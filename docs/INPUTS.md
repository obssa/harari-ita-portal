# Inputs needed from the Agency

Nothing on this list is guessed. Until an item arrives, the site shows clearly labelled sample content and
staging stays noindex. Tick each item when received and note where it is stored.

## Identity
- [ ] Official name in English, Amharic, Afaan Oromo and Harari (and the short name or acronym, if any)
- [ ] Logo (SVG preferred), the colour and mono versions, any brand guidelines, and the regional government emblem rules
- [ ] Mission, vision and values (official wording)
- [ ] Mandate: the establishing regulation or proclamation (number and date) and the powers and duties

## Organisation
- [ ] Organisation chart: directorates and teams, each with a one-line responsibility
- [ ] Leadership to publish: name, title, photo and short bio, with **written consent** for each person
- [ ] Office address, map location, hours, phone and hotline, official email, social media accounts

## Services and operations
- [ ] Services the Agency provides or supports. For each: who it is for, documents needed, steps, fees, time, the responsible directorate, and any online link
- [ ] IT support scope: which offices are served, ticket categories, target response and resolve times (SLA)
- [ ] Systems to show on the status page (names, owners)
- [ ] ICT standards and guidelines that may be published

## Innovation
- [ ] Current or planned programmes (incubation, acceleration), with eligibility and benefits
- [ ] Startups and innovators who agree to be listed
- [ ] Planned challenges or hackathons (dates, prizes, partners, judges with consent)
- [ ] How the Agency works with MInT on startup labelling (wording to use)

## Projects, data, skills
- [ ] Projects to publish: objectives, milestones, partners, status; whether budgets may be shown
- [ ] Figures for "Digital Harar in numbers", each with its source and owner (no number without a source)
- [ ] Datasets that may be published openly, with licence (for example CC BY 4.0) and update frequency
- [ ] Courses and trainings: syllabus, level, duration, venue, instructors, and the certificate design and signatory

## Legal and technical
- [ ] **Production hosting under the Personal Data Protection Proclamation 1321/2024**: where personal data may be stored (a host in Ethiopia or a written legal basis), and who signs off
- [ ] The privacy notice and terms of use, reviewed by the Agency's legal adviser
- [ ] The production domain (`*.gov.et`) and who manages its DNS
- [ ] SMS gateway account (Ethio Telecom) and the sender ID; official email sending (SMTP) and the sender address
- [ ] Fayda / eSignet client registration, if applicant sign-in with Fayda is wanted
- [ ] Partner logos, with permission to show them

## Heritage and imagery
- [ ] Confirmation of the five historic gate names and spellings (Jugol Heritage Conservation Office)
- [ ] Photographs the Agency owns or may use, with photographer credit and consent from the people shown
